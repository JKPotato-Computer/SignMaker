Welcome to SignMaker! WORK IN PROGRESS

SignMaker is a web-based program that allows you to make realistic highway signs that exist in the United States and Canada! We are based off the roots of Kurumi's Sign Maker, designed to be easy and efficient; only this time, with a whole lot more features. I hope you enjoy my little creation!

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • W Move road up 5px (Shift for 1px)
  • A Move road left 5px (Shift for 1px)
  • S Move road down 5px (Shift for 1px)
  • D Move road right 5px (Shift for 1px)
  • I Move road straight 5px (Shift for 1px)
  • O Move road backwards 5px (Shift for 1px)
  • Q / E Rotate 2∘ left / right (Shift for 1∘)
  • Arrow Left / Arrow Right Shift by 1 lane left / right
  • +Add new road segment
  • Delete / Backspace / -Delete selected road
  • Escape Deselect road or cancel moving
